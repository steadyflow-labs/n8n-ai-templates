Data handling

How we handle your purchase orders

Purchase orders carry customer names, addresses and prices. Before you send us any, here is exactly where they go, who sees them and when they are deleted.

During a pilot

  1. Black out what we don't need. Please remove bank details, card numbers, signatures and personal phone numbers before sending. The reader only needs the PO header, the ship-to and bill-to, and the lines.
  2. Kept in one place. They arrive at hello@plumblineautomation.com and are kept in a single private folder for the pilot. They are not shared with anyone outside Plumbline, and never with another client.
  3. How they are read. Each PO is read by a Claude model through Anthropic's commercial API. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, API inputs and outputs are not used to train models, and Anthropic deletes them from its systems within 30 days unless its usage policy or the law requires longer.
  4. Deleted after delivery. Once you have the CSVs, flags and accuracy report, we delete your files, the email they came in and our working copies, and confirm the deletion in writing.
  5. Never used for marketing without your written permission. If you are happy for us to describe the result as a case study, we will ask first and send you the text to approve.

After setup

The workflow runs in your accounts, not ours: your order inbox (Microsoft 365, Outlook or Gmail), your automation workspace (n8n, cloud or self-hosted) and your own Anthropic API key. That means:

  • POs go from your inbox to the model and back to your folder. They do not pass through our systems.
  • You pay the model costs directly, usually a few cents per document, and you can see every call in your own API console.
  • You can revoke our access the day the handover is done. Every step is documented in a written guide, so you are never dependent on us.
  • If your policy needs it, Anthropic offers zero data retention for eligible API accounts, subject to its approval.

What the reader will not do

  • Guess. An unreadable quantity, a missing item number or a price outside your list is flagged for a person, never filled in.
  • Send anything. It writes a CSV and a short list of flags. It does not email your customers, place orders or change your order system.
  • Keep a copy. There is no separate database of your POs on our side.
Questions about a specific policy or questionnaire? Email hello@plumblineautomation.com. We work in writing, so you will have every answer on file.
